# Provider icon sources

The following product icons were retrieved from the products' official websites on September 5, 2026. They identify supported services; trademarks belong to their owners.

- `yandexgpt.png`: Alice AI product icon, used for the YandexGPT/Alice model family. [Official website](https://alice.yandex.ru/); [original PNG](https://yastatic.net/s3/alicestatic/_/v2/static/media/android-icon.31ef51b3.png).
- `yandex-cloud.png`: Yandex Cloud provider icon. [Official brand page](https://yandex.cloud/en/brandbook); [original PNG](https://storage.yandexcloud.net/cloud-www-assets/main-icons/favicon/favicon-192x192.png).
- `gigachat.png`: GigaChat product icon. [Official website](https://giga.chat/landing); [original PNG](https://giga.chat/icons/gigachat/favicon-96x96.png).

These replace copies of the OpenAI image previously stored under the YandexGPT and GigaChat filenames. DeepSeek, Qwen and GPT-OSS use their existing model-family assets regardless of their hosting provider.
